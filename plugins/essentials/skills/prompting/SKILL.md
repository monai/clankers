## OpenAI

- `references/openai/gpt-6-astra.md` covers `gpt-6.1-sol`, `gpt-6-astra`, and `gpt-6-luna`. Its URL path is `latest-model/gpt-6-astra`.
- `references/openai/prompt-engineering.md` covers general prompt engineering.
- `references/openai/frontend-prompt.md` covers building frontends. It is written for GPT-5.5. Many of its patterns apply to later models.

Each `references/openai/<name>.md` is `https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/<name>.md`. A link to that page, with or without `.md` or a `#fragment`, means that file. Read the file, not the URL.
